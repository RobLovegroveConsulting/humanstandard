MEET ROB

Strategy. Context.
The right questions.

Rob works at the intersection of communication, digital strategy and data. His international career spans marketing, PR and advertising, with leadership experience in agencies and at board level, including executive responsibilities at Rowland.

He has worked with brands including Brisbane Airport, McDonald’s and Vodafone, as well as all three levels of Australian government. His experience covers brand positioning, campaigns, issues management and evaluation: understanding the situation, finding the useful insight and deciding what to do next.

Rob has also co-authored academic research on AI influencers and guest-lectured at the University of Queensland on data-led communication. At The Human Standard, that curiosity meets a practical question: how can this help your business do better work?

MEET SAM

Creative instinct.
A considered finish.

Sam brings creative direction, brand thinking and a strong sense of how work gets delivered. Her experience leading teams in complex, public-facing environments has shaped an approach that combines imagination with structure.

As Creative Director of our boutique creative and communication agency, she oversees work from brand creation through to implementation, including print materials and digital design. Her experience spans financial services, engineering, real estate, activewear and artisan food and beverage brands.

Sam takes time to understand the people behind a business and the connection they have to their brand. That care carries into the detail: how an idea looks, how it feels and how consistently it shows up. At The Human Standard, she helps keep creative work purposeful and true to the business it represents.
