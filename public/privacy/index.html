PRIVACY INFORMATION

Your information.

How we handle the details you share through this website.

Resource download requests

When you request a workbook or template pack, we collect your name, email address and the resource you selected. The form also records the page used to make the request. We use these details to manage resource requests and respond to questions about them. A download request does not subscribe you to marketing emails.

Enquiries

The enquiry form collects your name, email, business name, selected support option and message so The Human Standard can respond. Please do not include sensitive personal information or confidential customer material.

Storage and service providers

Netlify hosts this website and processes form submissions, including spam filtering. Form details are stored in the site's Netlify Forms account, where authorised account users can review or export them. Form submissions are not stored in the public GitHub website repository.

Website analytics and cookies

With your permission, we use Google Analytics to understand website visits, pages viewed and interactions such as outbound links and resource downloads. Google processes technical and usage information, including browser and device details and approximate location. Analytics cookies help distinguish visits. We do not send your name, email address or form message to Google Analytics, and advertising personalisation is disabled.

Analytics stays off until you choose Accept analytics. You can decline or change your choice at any time using Cookie preferences in the footer. Your choice is stored in your browser. Google may process analytics data overseas. See Google’s privacy policy (opens in a new tab).

Questions or requests

To ask about your information, or request access, correction or deletion, contact The Human Standard through the enquiry form. Include enough information for us to identify the relevant request, without sharing passwords or sensitive records.
