OUR APPROACH

Your business first.
Technology second.

Useful AI starts with an understanding of your business: what you stand for, who you serve and the work you need done. We build from there.

YOUR MARKETING & CREATIVE BRAIN

Your knowledge.
A shared foundation.

Generic instructions only take you so far. We organise approved information about your business into a practical foundation: brand voice, audiences, products, offers, goals and examples of good work.

Your agents and skills use this context to prepare more relevant, consistent drafts. This is a working knowledge foundation, rather than training a new AI model from scratch.

Specialist support, connected by context.

An agent is configured for a role or sequence of tasks using available tools. A skill provides reusable instructions for a particular job. We choose the approach that suits the task, platform and access you have.

The aim is a useful team: each part understands its job, uses the same approved brand knowledge and has clear points for human input.

01

Find the right first task.

We look at your goals, audiences, recurring jobs and existing tools. Together, we choose a task with a clear purpose: a more consistent social plan, a faster reporting draft or better-prepared customer communication. We agree what a useful result should look like before building.

02

Build it around you.

We organise your approved knowledge, develop the chosen agents or skills and define their instructions. Your voice and working practices shape the setup. We agree source access, what can be connected and which actions need approval.

03

Try it on real work.

We test against realistic examples, check whether the output is accurate and useful, and refine the instructions. Your team gets practical guidance on supplying a good brief, reviewing drafts and recognising when a question needs a person.

04

Keep it useful.

Each paid tier includes a defined level of ongoing human quality assurance and advice. We review agreed sample outputs or workflows, give practical feedback and refine the setup as your priorities change. Higher tiers provide broader review and closer strategic support.

Professional judgement.
At every step.

Speed matters when the work is right for your business. We review agreed outputs for clarity, consistency and suitability. Review covers the scope we agree with you; every AI output is not automatically checked. Your business keeps final approval over what is used or published.
